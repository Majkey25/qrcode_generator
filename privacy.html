Privacy & terms

Updated 5 October 2026. QR Maker is maintained by Matěj Teplý, publishing as Majkey. Contact: majkeylab@gmail.com.

Your QR content

Text, URLs and selected logo files are processed in your browser. The app does not upload them, create an account or save them in browser storage. Closing the page clears the app's working content; downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them. Browser history, form restoration and backups are controlled by your browser.

Hosting and external services

GitHub Pages serves this site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. The QR library is loaded from jsDelivr, so that provider receives a network request including your IP address and browser request information. The app does not send your QR text or selected logo with that request. Provider retention and international transfers follow their own notices: GitHub privacy statement and jsDelivr privacy policy.

Cookies and tracking

The app sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or persistent browser storage. External websites you choose to visit have their own policies.

Contact and data requests

Support correspondence is processed for the legitimate interest of answering enquiries. Requests to exercise privacy rights are handled to meet applicable legal obligations. Sending an enquiry is optional; it is not needed to generate a QR code.

Email the contact above for privacy, access, correction or deletion requests. Do not include passwords or sensitive QR content. Correspondence is used to handle your request and retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. Where applicable, you can object to processing, request restriction or complain to your data protection authority. The maintainer cannot delete files on your device or provider-controlled logs; use the provider's privacy contact for those logs.

Use, price and refunds

This tool is free. It has no purchases, subscriptions or refund transactions. Use only content and logos you have permission to use. Test the downloaded code with a scanner before printing or sharing it; custom colours, styles and logos can affect readability. No guarantee is made that every scanner will read every design. These terms do not limit rights that cannot be excluded by law.
